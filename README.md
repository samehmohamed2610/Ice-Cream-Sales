# Ice Cream Sales Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze ice cream sales performance, branch operations, product demand, employee contribution, discount behavior, and time-based business trends.

## Project Overview

This project presents a complete business intelligence solution for an ice cream sales dataset. The dashboard helps users understand overall sales performance, identify top-performing branches and products, analyze employee contribution, and explore seasonal and time-based sales patterns.

The report was designed with multiple interactive pages, clean navigation, hidden filter panels using bookmarks, and drillthrough functionality for deeper analysis.

## Dashboard Pages

### Navigation Page

The dashboard starts with a clean navigation page that allows users to move between the main report sections:

- Executive Dashboard
- Branch Operations
- Product & Inventory
- Sales & Time Analysis

### Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance.

Key insights include:

- Total Sales: 299K
- Total Quantity Sold: 9K
- Average Order Value: 116.31
- Top Branch: Cairo
- Top Product: Cone

This page analyzes total sales by branch, payment method, product, and monthly trends across 2023, 2024, and 2025.

### Branch Operations

The Branch Operations page compares performance across Cairo, Unknown, Mansoura, Tanta, and Alex.

Key insights include:

- Cairo achieved the highest sales with 101,670
- Cairo contributed 34.00% of total branch sales
- Cairo recorded 2,953 total quantity sold
- Average sales per branch reached 59.81K

This page also includes branch ranking, average order value by branch, discount rate by branch, and city-level sales distribution.

### Product & Inventory

The Product & Inventory page focuses on product-level performance, gross sales, quantity sold, pricing behavior, and category contribution.

Key insights include:

- Top Product: Cone
- Cone generated 90,430 in total sales
- Cone contributed 30.24% of total category sales
- Ice Cream Cup ranked second with 86,835 in sales
- Ice Cream Cup contributed 29.04% of category sales
- Gross Sales reached 308K

The page also includes price versus quantity analysis to compare product demand and pricing performance.

### Sales & Time Analysis

The Sales & Time Analysis page evaluates employee sales performance and seasonal trends.

Key insights include:

- Total Employees: 7
- Average Sales per Employee: 42.72K
- Discounted Invoices: 46.25%
- Top Employee: Ahmed
- Ahmed generated 75,865 in total sales
- Winter was the top season with 78K in sales

This page compares total sales by employee, sales by season, and monthly sales contribution by employee.

### Drillthrough Analysis

The project includes a drillthrough page for deeper time-based analysis. Users can drill into sales by:

- Year
- Quarter
- Month
- Day of week

Key insights from the drillthrough page include:

- Friday was the top day of week
- Winter was the top season
- October was the top month
- Year-over-year growth was analyzed through a dedicated KPI visual

## Interactive Features

### Bookmarks

Bookmarks were used to create a hidden filter panel. This allowed filters and slicers to stay accessible without taking space from the main dashboard layout.

This improved the user experience by keeping the dashboard clean, focused, and easier to navigate.

### Drillthrough

Drillthrough was used to let users move from summary-level insights into more detailed time-based analysis. This makes it easier to investigate sales performance by year, quarter, month, and day of week.
